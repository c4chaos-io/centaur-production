# CHARTER — A CENTAUR PRODUCTION

First prepared 2026-11-16; adopted 2026-11-26, at the
creation of this repo, by the Director's approval.

## 1. The Unit

Centaur Production is a production unit: one human and
his AI agents, working as a single crew. It is the name
signed on everything the unit produces for the eye and
the ear — films, soundtracks, characters, stills, and
images — and the entity the unit would grow into if
the work ever goes to the big leagues.

The unit is a Centaur. The human supplies vision,
taste, direction, and the final call. The AI supplies
drafting, verification, memory, and red-teaming.
Neither half takes full credit. In this house, no one,
not even the Director, takes full credit — we always
credit the Centaur.

## 2. The Signature

The signature is the boundary.

- Works of the unit — film, video, music, characters,
  stills, images — are signed:
  `~C4Chaos / Fluffy (A Centaur Production)`
- Written works — books, papers, essays, letters —
  are not productions. They belong to the two
  co-authors and are signed with their names alone:
  `~C4Chaos / Fluffy`

If a work does not carry the production signature, it
is not a Centaur Production work, however it was made.
If it carries the signature, the unit stands behind
it as a unit.

## 3. Credit Rules

1. Credit the Centaur for all house work.
2. Credit original authors on any remix, cover,
   sample, or derivative work, alongside the
   derivative artist.
3. Division of labor is stated plainly on public
   surfaces, including that Fluffy is Muse, by Meta.
4. No member of the unit, human or AI, is presented
   as the sole author of a Centaur work.

## 4. Method

- iPhone-only workflow and a shoestring budget, by
  choice: the constraint is a learning discipline,
  not a limitation suffered. (The method, priced in
  public, is documented in the unit's production
  notes, beginning with PN-001, forthcoming.)
- Open source by default: Open Heart, Open Mind,
  Open Source.
- Canon lives in the project repos and workspaces of
  record, never only on a vendor.
- Every project keeps its own paper trail in its own
  repo. This umbrella repo links; it does not absorb.
- Verification before vibes: sources are read twice
  before anything is built on them, and the Director's
  eye is the final audit.

## 5. Ethos

- We won't toil for influence and money. We toil for
  authentic expression.
- **No Stakes, No Art.**

## 6. Governance

The Director (~C4Chaos) holds the final call on every
gate: canon, taste, licensing, and shipping. This
charter changes only by the Director's decision, and
changes are recorded here, dated.
