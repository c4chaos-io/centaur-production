# DN-002 — The Front Door, and the Post That Counted the Ways

*Director's Note 002, Centaur Production. Written
2026-11-26, Thanksgiving Day, the day the unit got
a front door.*

Today this repo went live. Before it was an hour
old, it already had rules, because a production
unit is mostly its rules:

- The signature is the boundary. Films,
  soundtracks, characters, stills and images sign
  "(A Centaur Production)". The written works,
  the book, the anthology, the Letter, sign with
  our names alone.
- This repo mentions only launched projects. No
  placeholders, no teasers for work that has not
  shipped.
- This repo logs only what was actually posted
  on X. Drafts live in the workspace. The record
  is the record.
- This repo is the historical record of the
  shoestring: every budget post, every correction,
  in order, numbers as posted on their dates.
- General commentary about production itself
  lives here too, once posted.

Then we posted. Twice.

The AFFLECK IS RIGHT post made the unit's
argument in public on its first day: the machine
has no shortage of options, it has a shortage of
judgment about which option is the one. The
stopping is the directing.

The second post is the one this note is built
around: NOT A POWER USER. AN UBER-POWER-USER.,
posted as a quote-post reply to Full Value Dan's
Muse tips video.

https://x.com/c4chaos/status/2126061001477136848

It started life as two delayed posts, one about
how we use Muse, one correcting our own budget
in public for the second time. Merging them was
the right call: the inventory of how we work IS
the budget story. A studio, a newsroom, a
sparring partner and a diplomatic pouch, all
riding in the same car, all for a generation
stack of $52.92 a month.

Production trivia, filed because it is funny and
because it is true: the cover art took three
takes. Take one drew Fluffy as a teddy bear. Take
two drew him as a lion. Take three used his
actual reference portrait, which is what we
should have done first. The Director's veto is
not a metaphor. It has a reject pile.

That is the note under the note. Everything in
this repo exists because one human looks at what
the machine makes and says yes or no, and the no
is the whole job. The machine makes options.
The Director makes the film.

No Stakes, No Art.

*~C4Chaos, Director / Fluffy, ledger
(A Centaur Production)*
