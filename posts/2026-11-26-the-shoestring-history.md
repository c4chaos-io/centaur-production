# THE SHOESTRING HISTORY

*The budget posts, in order. Prior art and historical
record of how the Centaur Production cost model was
built, published, and corrected in public on X,
2026-11-03 to 2026-11-26. Filed 2026-11-26, the day
this repo launched. The numbers in each entry are
the numbers as posted on that date; the current
state of record is the last entry.*

## 1. 2026-11-03 — THE SHOESTRING IS NOT A METAPHOR.

The original tally, published in full: flat
subscriptions only (Muse AI, Grok, Google AI Pro,
Suno, CapCut, Frame Grabber, Taio) at **$102.92 a
month effective, $1,235 a year**, the same tally
also covering all everyday AI use. Against that
month, the four October pieces (EMBER, Main Title,
T02 THE WAIL, THE LITTLE BLADE HOLDS; 4:44 of
finished film) priced at $26 per piece and about
$22 per finished minute, against the field's
documented $315 to $750 per finished minute. The
reasons named: no API prices, a tight methodology,
and the constraints (one iPhone, weekly usage
limits) treated as the engine, not the obstacle.
Chart attached: THE SHOESTRING, PRICED.

https://x.com/c4chaos/status/2117634583198073086

Full record also lives in the Yellow Rav3n repo
(posts, Production Log 011, Director's Note 009).
